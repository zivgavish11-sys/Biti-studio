# Series: "הבית בעוד 30 שנה" (followers' robot home) — agreed with Ziv 2026-11-15 in the boss chat
Replaces the "המעבדה של העוקבים" lab series (no one signed up; Ziv wants a fresh concept focused on getting follows).

## Mechanic
- Every **10 followers, counted across TikTok + Instagram + Facebook together**, a new robot enters the home.
- Each robot has a charging station with **10 name plates**. Names appear **only for people who follow AND comment "בית"** (the comment = consent). Never put names of followers who didn't comment.
- In the same comment people vote between **two options** for the next robot; most votes wins.
- **Ziv collects the names and votes himself** from the comments (community agent stays paused) and gives them to the video agent for the next part. Never invent names.
- No Biti on screen and no "אני ביטי" line. No brands (autonomous car = generic, no logo).
