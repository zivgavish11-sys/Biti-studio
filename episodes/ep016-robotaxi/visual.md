# ep016-robotaxi — visual script v1 (hero look, one world, x-ray car)
World: one street — parked jeep on the right, sidewalk, generic robotaxi (vehicles/robotaxi, NO Tesla logo/shape replica, roof lidar hidden — cameras only) driving toward camera. Indigo + light-blue accent.
1. Hook (0–3 s): high 3/4, the car rolling; a small lilac child mannequin starts running out from behind the jeep — freeze-ish slow motion. Title "מי בולם מהר יותר? | אתם או רכב אוטונומי".
2. "אין לייזר ואין רדאר. רק מצלמות.": car shell goes see-through; a lidar and radar icon pop and get crossed out; camera pods on all sides light up light-blue.
3. "מצלמות מכל צדי הרכב": thin view cones from every camera fan out; small frame thumbnails flicker (tens per second).
4. "למוח אחד, רשת עצבית": cables run from cameras to a computer box inside; above it a floating screen with a glowing-dots neural net (matte, no glow) filling up.
5. "למדה מהמון סרטונים": the screen flips through many tiny driving clips into the net.
6. "מעריכה מרחק": depth colouring on the street (near = light, far = dark) + a distance tag on the jeep.
7. "ילד קופץ... מנחשת לאן הוא רץ": back to the child; dashed predicted path arrow across the road crossing the car's path; collision point marked.
8. "אף אחד לא כתב לה חוק": re-hook shift — a rule card "אם ילד → תבלמי" appears and is wiped away; the net screen lights a single path to "לבלום".
9. DECISION → BRAKE, x-ray close-up (Ziv's key shot, slow and clear):
   a. pulse travels as a light-blue dot along the wire from the computer to the brake unit (callout "אות חשמלי");
   b. small motor turns and pushes a piston (callout "מנוע");
   c. brake fluid flows in the lines toward all four wheels (animated dashes in the pipes);
   d. at the wheel: caliper squeezes pads onto the disc, disc slows (callout "רפידות + דיסק"); a "בלי רגל" tag with the empty pedal.
10. "לנהג אנושי... שנייה": split timeline bar: car's chain short vs a human chain (eyes → brain → foot) one second long; car stops before the child's path.
11. Outro card "תעקבו", composition echoes frame 0 for the loop.
Assets needed (media): x-ray car internals — computer box, wiring, brake booster motor + master cylinder, fluid lines, 4 discs + calipers + pads (animatable), empty brake pedal; child mannequin (small, running anim); parked jeep/SUV generic; street set (reuse citycorner); camera view cones (fx-city viewCone), neural-net dots screen, timeline bar.
