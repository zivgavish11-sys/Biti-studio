# ep028-price — visual script v1 (frame0: eye). Robots generic, no brands/replicas. All text fully visible. Room for Biti at hook, twist, end card.
1. Hook (0–3 s), eye level: two humanoids side by side on one stage — a small child-height one mid-handstand, a tall one in a soft knit suit holding a glass. Price tags above them "6,000$" / "20,000$". Top text: "6,000$ מול 20,000$". On "נבנה לעבוד אצלכם בבית" a small home icon lights next to the tall one.
2. "על מה משלמים" — rise to the high 3/4 wide, both robots + tags.
3. Joints — the tall robot goes x-ray; joints light up one by one, a counter fills ("עשרות מפרקים"); close-up callout on one joint: motor · transmission · sensor (behind the joint).
4. The cheap one — a height line next to a child silhouette (~1.2 m); it does a cartwheel; a small laptop screen "למפתחים".
5. The expensive one — height line next to an adult silhouette (~1.65 m), soft suit; fly into the hand: 20+ joint dots, tendons from the forearm; fingers close gently on a glass, a grip gauge stays light blue (no break).
6. Re-hook (stage darkens): the tall robot at a front door; a card "הזמנה מוקדמת · מנוי חודשי" like a car-lease contract.
7. Mid follow line "עוד הסברים כאלה? תעקבו" 1.5 s, caption style, centre.
8. "בעיקר בארצות הברית" — a flat map, US lit, Israel dim; then a price graph line sloping down, dashed (= forecast).
9. End card: the question "הייתם לוקחים רובוט במנוי? כן או לא" + follow button (visual only, not spoken). Last frame echoes frame 0 (two robots side by side).
