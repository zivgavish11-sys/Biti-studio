# ep021-dronedelivery — visual script v1 (hero look, one world = Tel-Aviv neighbourhood kit from media; camera flies)
Accent light blue #8ea6ff = signal/line; deeper blue #4f6fe0 = force (fans, thrust).
1. 0–4.2 s hook (approved draft): hand up on a Tel-Aviv rooftop, parcel above on a line → camera rides the line looking down, parcel + person shrink, counter 0→100 מ׳ → over the drone, sea + promenade below.
2. "אז למה הרחפן לא פשוט נוחת?" — camera dives from the drone toward the street; a ghost drone tries to land, a red-free "✕" ring (light blue) on the spot.
3. "עצים, חוטי חשמל, ואנשים." — three quick zooms with callouts on the street: tree canopy, electric wires between poles, people on the sidewalk (lilac mannequins).
4. "אז הוא נשאר גבוה, ומוריד חבל." — back up: drone hovering high, winch turns, line unspools (light blue).
5. "אבל הנה החלק המטורף: ... יש רובוט קטן." — re-hook shift: camera drops along the line to its end: the parcel pod opens a little — it's a small droid (fins + small fans), callout "רובוט".
6. "יש לו מדחפים... בדיוק למקום. אפילו לשולחן בחצר." — the droid's fans spin (deeper blue), it slides sideways under the trees to a garden table; a light-blue target ring on the table, droid lands on it exactly.
7. "הוא משאיר את החבילה, החבל נגלל בחזרה..." — parcel stays on the table, droid reels up the line fast, camera follows up; drone tilts and flies off over the roofs.
8. "ובלי לצלצל בפעמון." — joke beat: cut to the front door, a doorbell button with a small "zzz"; nobody rang.
9. outro "עוד הסברים על רובוטים? תעקבו." — high shot echoing frame 0's end: drone over Tel-Aviv, card + Follow.
