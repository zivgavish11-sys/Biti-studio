# ep024-drowsy — visual script (pro-v3 test #2, frame0 eye-level test #1). Ziv approved 2026-11-25.
World: one night Israeli highway (dark asphalt, white lane dashes, streetlights) + the car cabin. Camera flies between cabin ↔ outside. Accent #8ea6ff = signal (sensor, graph, wire); #4f6fe0 = force (the jerk). Key words red: חיישן · משיכה · התראה.
1. hook (0–3 s) — EYE LEVEL from the passenger seat: grey driver silhouette, hands on the wheel, head dips a little; highway lights stream through the windshield. On-screen title: "האוטו יודע שאתם נרדמים?"
2. "אז איך הוא יודע?" — camera backs out through the windshield and rises → high 3/4 wide: the car alone on the night highway.
3. sensor — dive back into the cabin; steering column turns x-ray ghost, small sensor in the column lights light-blue; callout "חיישן זווית" + angle arc on the wheel.
4. micro-corrections — top-down on the wheel: tiny left/right wiggles; floating screen (dashed line to the column) draws a smooth wiggly graph.
5. learns — graph compresses into a band labelled "ככה אתם נוהגים ערניים"; small clock 0→5 דק'.
6. re-hook "אבל הנה החלק המטורף:" — cabin light dims (visual shift), slow push toward the driver's head, it nods.
7. hands almost stop — graph goes almost flat; outside high 3/4: car drifts slowly toward the lane line.
8. the jerk — wheel snaps (deeper blue pulse), graph spike; car snaps back into the lane.
9. "לא צריכה לראות אתכם" — close on hands + wheel, wire from the sensor to a computer under the dash, pulse runs along it.
10. compare — screen: the alert band vs flat+spike line, the mismatch circled with a ring.
11. alert — dashboard cluster close-up: coffee-cup icon + "הגיע הזמן להפסקה?"
12. outro card — back to the frame-0 composition (eye level, driver), card shrink + Follow.
Assets needed (asked media 2026-11-25): car cabin with rotatable steering wheel, x-ray column + angle sensor, dash computer + wire path, cluster 'screen', driver mannequin seated (hands on wheel, head nod), night highway strip.
