# ep015-pokemon — visual script v1 (hero look, one world, camera flies)
World: one city corner — sidewalk + road (street/Street_4Way), 2–3 buildings, a small plaza with a statue, deliveryBot on the sidewalk. Indigo, one accent (light blue). No Pokémon, no logos.
1. 0–3 s hook: high 3/4, robot rolling on the sidewalk; in front a phone scanning the statue (light-blue scan frame on screen). Title on screen.
2. "איך זה קשור?": camera swoops from the phone down to the robot.
3. "חייב לדעת בדיוק איפה הוא": close on the robot, its screen: "איפה אני?" + pin.
4. "לוויין טועה": crane up; small satellite above, dashed line down; floating map screen, blue dot jumping, error circle of a few metres spilling onto the road. Callout "טעות של כמה מטרים".
5. "לזהות בעיניים": zoom into the robot's cameras, view cone to the building facade.
6. "תמונות של כל פינה": photo frames pop around the corner building from many angles; light changes (sun → cloud).
7. "מי יצלם את כל זה...": pull back, empty quiet street (beat).
8. "הטוויסט: אתם.": re-hook shift — lilac mannequins with phones appear around the statue.
9. "המשחק שלח... פרסים": phone screen in Hebrew "משימה: סרקו את הפסל" + reward star; scan rings around the statue; small photo frames fly up.
10. "30 מיליארד תמונות": frames stream from all over the city into one cloud; counter rolls to 30,000,000,000.
11. "מפה... משווה... סנטימטרים": the city turns into a dot map; camera returns to the robot; camera-view lines snap onto building edges, pin locks, error circle shrinks to a dot. Callout "לפי החברה: סנטימטרים".
12. outro card: scene shrinks into the card, Follow button; composition echoes frame 0 for the loop.
Assets: statue + satellite → media request (or primitives); deliveryBot, street, buildings, smartphone, humanoid-as-mannequin exist.
