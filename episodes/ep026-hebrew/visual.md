# ep026-hebrew — visual script v1 (pro-v3 look, test video 4/4; frame0: eye)
World: one Israeli kitchen/living room (home2056/livingRoom + kitchenRun), realistic humanoid (pro look), person = grey silhouette on the sofa. Light: pro-v3 studio/daylight, darker (no milky frames).
1. Hook (0–3 s) — eye level 1.6 m: humanoid by an open window, holding a crumpled empty bag, arm half-raised toward the window. Pedal bin across the room. Two dashed arcs from the bag: one into the bin, one out of the window. Text: 'תעיף את זה'?
2. "איך הוא יודע" — camera rises to the high 3/4 wide shot: whole room, the person on the sofa, a sound wave runs from the person to the robot.
3. Voice → text — fly to the robot's head: the wave enters, and a floating screen next to the head (dashed line) types "תעיף את זה".
4. "מילים זה לא מספיק" — the word "תעיף" splits into two cards: bin icon "לזרוק" / window icon "להעיף באוויר". Both arcs flash again.
5. Context — pull back; three callouts light up one by one: a camera view cone on the bag ("מה המצלמות רואות"), a chat bubble from earlier ("מה אמרתם קודם"), a pile of text ("מה אנשים מתכוונים"). The bin card gets stronger.
6. Learned from text — thousands of text pages stream into the robot's head.
7. Re-hook (visual shift, stage goes dark) — two stacks: a giant English tower vs a small Hebrew stack; on "וסלנג?" a thin sliver on top (סבבה, יאללה, תעיף).
8. Payoff — back in the room: the robot stops, arm down, a screen above it: "לזרוק לפח?"; the person nods; the robot drops the bag in the bin.
9. Outro — grouper card + Follow; the last frame echoes frame 0 (robot by the window).
