# ep027-chores — visual script v1 (frame0: eye; look: per Ziv after the pro-v3 test — default pro-v3 until decided)
One world: an Israeli house cutaway (living room + kitchen) with a backyard (pool + lawn) and a Tel-Aviv apartment block across the street. All text fully visible (Ziv rule). Leave empty room for Biti at the hook, the twist and the end card.
1. Hook (0–3 s), eye level: crumpled T-shirt on the sofa arm in front; a robot vacuum passes under it leaving a wet shine; through the glass door the pool bot works in the water. Top text: "מה רובוט באמת יודע לעשות?"
2. "אז מה רובוט באמת יודע" — rise to the high 3/4 wide: the whole house + yard, every robot working at once.
3. Vacuum — fly in: it docks, the dock sucks the dust out of its bin, the mop pads spin in a wash tray. ✓ tag.
4. Window — the camera crosses the street to the apartment block: the window bot on a 10th-floor window, a floor counter "קומה 10". ✓
5. Pool — the pool bot climbs the pool wall up to the waterline. ✓
6. Lawn — the mower cuts stripes and rolls back into its dock, a charge ring fills. ✓
7. "מה עוד לא באמת" — back inside, three spots with ✗: laundry pile, open dishwasher with dirty dishes, cold pan on the cooktop. On "דמויי אדם כבר מנסים": a humanoid holds a shirt, a dashed line from it to a distant grey silhouette with a headset ("עוד לא לבד").
8. Re-hook (stage darkens, one spotlight): a vacuum unfolds a small arm and lifts a sock off the floor.
9. Mid follow line: "עוד הסברים כאלה? תעקבו" for 1.5 s, caption style, centre (media will design the final look).
10. "איטי, לא תמיד מצליח" — a second sock slips from the gripper; then a line on the floor between the ✓ side and the ✗ side slides toward the ✗ side ("הגבול זז").
11. Comment ask — three ✗ cards "כביסה · מדיח · בישול" with a comment icon; the end card + Follow button over this line. Last frame echoes frame 0 (sofa + shirt).
Ziv approved visual v1 (2026-11-25); length ~52 s accepted (option A).
