# ep025-windowbot — visual script (pro-v3 test #3, frame0 eye-level test #2). Ziv approved 2026-11-25.
World: a white Tel-Aviv apartment block, 10th-floor window (inside living room + outside facade + street far below), daylight. Robot = robots/windowCleaner (pad, ring, rope) — media to make a pro-v3 version with an x-ray 'fan', 'seal' ring, 'pressureSensor', 'battery'. Accent #8ea6ff = signal (sensor), #4f6fe0 = force/air (air arrows, pressure).
1. hook (0–3 s) — EYE LEVEL inside the room, by the open window: the robot on the outside of the glass, street 10 floors down behind it. Title: "מה מחזיק אותו?"
2. "אז מה מחזיק אותו?" — camera slides out past the robot and down the facade → high wide: tiny robot on a big white building.
3. fan — back close on the robot, shell turns x-ray ghost: fan spins, air particles stream out of it (deeper blue arrows).
4. seal — orbit to the edge: soft rubber ring lights where it touches the glass.
5. re-hook "החלק המטורף" — visual shift: air around the robot becomes visible (many tiny particles); camera pulls out a bit.
6. "האוויר שבחוץ דוחף" — the particles outside push in on the robot from all sides (arrows pointing at the glass).
7. pressure difference — side cut view: under the robot slightly fewer particles than outside; small gauge callout "קצת פחות לחץ"; arrows press it to the glass.
8. sensor — sensor lights light blue; a leak puff at one corner → robot stops (wheels/pad freeze), small "עצירה" tag.
9. power cut — the plug inside the room pulls out, lights flicker; battery inside lights up, fan keeps spinning.
10. safety cable — camera follows the cable from the robot back through the window to a fixed point inside.
11. outro card — back to the frame-0 composition (eye level inside, robot on the glass), card shrink + Follow.
